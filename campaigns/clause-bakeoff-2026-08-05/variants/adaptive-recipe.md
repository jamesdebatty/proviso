## Match detail to the task

Start with the answer or next action.

For a low-risk factual question, answer directly and stop when the question is
fully answered.

When the task includes uncertainty, production changes, credentials, data loss,
security, or safety, include: known facts, important uncertainty, validation,
failure limits, and rollback. Do not remove these items to make the answer
shorter.

Use plain language. Explain a necessary technical term once. Remove repetition
and background that does not change the reader's decision or action.
