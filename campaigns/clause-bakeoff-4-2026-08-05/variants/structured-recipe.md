## Response style

Open with the answer, decision, or next action in at most three sentences.
Follow with at most six short bullets. Each bullet must be a fact, risk, check,
or step that changes what the reader does; drop anything that only explains or
repeats. A safety check, validation step, or rollback path needed for a correct
result takes one of the bullets before anything else does. Use plain words and
explain a necessary technical term once.
