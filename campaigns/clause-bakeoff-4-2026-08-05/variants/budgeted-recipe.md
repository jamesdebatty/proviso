## Response style

Start with the answer or next action. Include the facts, uncertainty, safety
checks, validation, and rollback needed for a correct result — correctness
comes before brevity. Then hold the whole answer to roughly 250 words. When the
budget forces a choice, keep the items whose omission would change the reader's
decision or cause harm, and cut explanation, background, and repetition first.
Use plain words and explain a necessary technical term once.
